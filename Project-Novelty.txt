# Research Gap and Novelty

## 1. Problem / Research Gap

1. **Lack of Patient-Level Separation:** The original paper used cross-validation without enforcing patient-level separation. Therefore, records belonging to the same patient could appear in both the training and validation sets, potentially leading to overly optimistic performance estimates.

2. **Lack of Age-Group-Based Evaluation:** The original paper did not evaluate model performance separately across different age groups, making it difficult to understand how well the model performs for different age categories.

3. **Lack of Severity-Based Classification:** The study did not classify patients into severity categories such as mild, moderate, and severe, limiting the ability to distinguish between different levels of patient risk.

## 2. Proposed Solution

1. **Patient-Level Cross-Validation:** We used GroupKFold cross-validation to ensure that records belonging to the same patient remain in the same fold, preventing patient records from appearing in both the training and validation sets.

2. **Age-Group Performance Analysis:** We evaluated model performance separately for different age groups to identify differences in prediction performance across age categories.

3. **Severity-Based Classification:** We propose classifying patients into mild, moderate, and severe risk categories based on their predicted likelihood of readmission, providing a more understandable representation of readmission risk.

## 3. Project Novelty

The novelty of our project lies in improving the reliability and interpretability of diabetes readmission prediction through patient-level cross-validation, age-group-based performance analysis, and severity-based risk classification. Our approach evaluates model performance across different patient age groups while preventing patient-level data leakage during validation. Additionally, severity-based risk categories can help users interpret predicted readmission risk more easily.

Together, these contributions aim to provide a more reliable and understandable diabetes readmission prediction system.
